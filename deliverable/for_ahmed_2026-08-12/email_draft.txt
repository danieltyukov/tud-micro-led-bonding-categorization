One result worth putting in the text. The chain ranking tracks the LED yield ranking
closely, Spearman rho = -0.92, p = 0.003. Conditions 5 and 7 have the lowest chain
resistance and were also the only two with no failed LED channels. The spread across
conditions is 0.39 ohm over 12 joints, roughly 30 mohm per joint, against a reported
deviation of 11 to 20 mohm. So the chain resolves bond resistance, which the LED series
resistance never could: re-seating the jumpers alone moved that by 0.84 ohm. Two
independent structures agreeing puts the process window on much firmer ground than either
one on its own.

One thing reading your ECTC paper turned up. The draft gives the Au effective resistivity
as 10e-8 ohm.m. The ECTC paper, same instrument and same group, gives 2.86e-8 for Au
(3.59e-8 Cu, 2.74e-8 Ag). Bulk gold is 2.44e-8, so 2.86 looks right and 10 is four times
bulk. Worth checking against the raw ResMap output, since the two numbers will sit close
together in the same paper.

attached file.

- The shunt fraction is about 44 %, not one third and not 61 %. Our meter sources 1.42 mA

- The re-seating variance share is 85 %. The draft has both 83 % and 85 %. The 83 % comes
