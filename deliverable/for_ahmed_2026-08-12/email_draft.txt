One result worth putting in the text. The chain ranking tracks the LED yield ranking
closely, Spearman rho = -0.92, p = 0.003. Conditions 5 and 7 have the lowest chain
resistance and were also the only two with no failed LED channels. The spread across
conditions is 0.39 ohm over 12 joints, roughly 30 mohm per joint, against a reported
deviation of 11 to 20 mohm. So the chain resolves bond resistance, which the LED series
resistance never could: re-seating the jumpers alone moved that by 0.84 ohm. Two
independent structures agreeing puts the process window on much firmer ground than either
one on its own.

Three numbers I would change before this goes out. The arithmetic is in section 3 of the
attached file.

- The shunt fraction is about 44 %, not one third and not 61 %. Our meter sources 1.42 mA
  in diode mode, measured directly as 0.142 V across a 100 ohm 0.1 % reference, not 1 mA.
  At the 1.781 V reading the shunt takes 0.62 mA of that. My original "one third" was wrong
  too. Separately, the paragraph starting "One numerical correction:" is an editing note in
  the first person and is still sitting in the body.

- The re-seating variance share is 85 %. The draft currently has both 83 % and 85 %. The
  83 % comes from rounding 0.84 before squaring.

- Self-heating: 200 K/W with -2 mV/K gives 0.80 ohm, not 1.2 ohm, so the agreement "to
  within 25 %" does not hold as written. Cleaner to invert it, since the measured 1.49 ohm
  implies about 370 K/W, which is ordinary for a 0404 package on two-layer FR-4. The
  conclusion is unaffected: heating sets in between 20 and 80 ms, so 5 ms is safe.
