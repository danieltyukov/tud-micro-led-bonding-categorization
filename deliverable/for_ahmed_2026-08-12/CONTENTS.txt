daisy_chain_figure.m
    Standalone MATLAB script that regenerates both figures from the CSV, so you can
    restyle or resize without coming back to me. Put it next to the CSV and run it.
    Needs nothing but base MATLAB.


Provenance, worth keeping straight in the text
    These chains are a separate experiment on the v1 board, not the LED coupons. Any
    comparison against the LED yield is a comparison between assembly conditions across
    two test vehicles, and it assumes the condition numbering is common to both. The v2
    board carries daisy chains as well, but none were measured on it.
