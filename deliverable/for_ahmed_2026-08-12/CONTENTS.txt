    Total daisy chain resistance against assembly condition, mean with deviation bars.

figures/daisy_chain_resistance_bars.png
figures/daisy_chain_resistance_bars.pdf
    Same data as bars. Pick whichever fits the layout; do not use both.

    Suggested caption:
    Total daisy chain resistance for each assembly condition, measured on the v1 board
    across a chain of six 1 x 1 mm2 Au-coated dummy dies. Markers are the mean, bars the
    reported deviation.

daisy_chain_resistance.csv
    The plotted numbers, from the workbook of 12 August 2026.
    Columns: condition, R_total_ohm, R_dev_ohm.

    These are a separate experiment on the v1 board, not the LED coupons. Any comparison
    with the LED yield is a comparison between assembly conditions across two test
    vehicles, and it assumes the condition numbering is common to both.
