CORRECTIONS

  Shunt fraction. The meter sources 1.42 mA in diode mode, not 1 mA. That was measured
  directly as 0.142 V across a 100 ohm 0.1 % reference. At the 1.781 V reading the shunt
  carries 0.62 mA and the junction gets 0.80 mA, so the shunt takes about 44 %. The
  paragraph beginning "One numerical correction:" was an editing note in the first person
  and has been deleted; its 61 % assumed a 1 mA source. The measurement setup table and
  the reverse bias subsection now say 1.42 mA as well.

  Variance share. The draft had both 83 % and 85 %. From the unrounded standard
  deviations, 0.843 squared over 0.9147 squared is 84.9 %, so 85 %.

  Self-heating. The stated inputs of 200 K/W and -2 mV/K give 0.80 ohm, not 1.2, so the
  agreement to within 25 % did not hold. I inverted the calculation instead: the measured
  1.49 ohm implies a junction-to-board thermal resistance of about 370 K/W, or an 11 K
  rise at 28.4 mW. That is ordinary for a 0404 package on two-layer FR-4. The conclusion
  is unchanged, heating sets in between 20 and 80 ms and 5 ms is safe.
